Overview

Metro 2033 is a post-apocalyptic franchise created by Dmitry Glukhovsky. Set in the ruins of Moscow's metro system after a nuclear war, it follows the struggles of survivors as they navigate mutated creatures and hostile human factions.

The franchise spans novels, video games, and various media adaptations, blending survival horror, action, and deep storytelling.

Games

Metro 2033 (2010)

The first game adaptation of the novel, focusing on stealth and survival in the dark tunnels of the Moscow Metro.

Metro: Last Light (2013)

Sequel to Metro 2033, expanding the story, combat, and survival horror elements.

Metro Exodus (2019)

The third installment, introducing open-world exploration while maintaining the series' dark, atmospheric tone.

Books

Metro 2033

The original novel by Dmitry Glukhovsky, setting the stage for the post-apocalyptic universe.

Metro 2034

Sequel novel exploring different characters and events in the Metro universe.

Metro 2035

Continuation of the story, connecting the novels more closely to the events of the games.

Media

The Metro franchise has inspired comics, art, and upcoming film adaptations, expanding the dark and atmospheric lore of the series.
